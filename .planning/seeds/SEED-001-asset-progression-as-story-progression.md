# SEED-001: Asset-progression-as-story-progression with diegetic damage + "grown not built" lore + mixed-mode character/age-driven distribution

## Why This Matters

This seed elevates an environmental-art flourish into a potential **core meta-loop driver**: as the wisp completes villager arcs, magic returns to Sterling's world, and grown-not-built infrastructure (lampposts, interior heirloom furniture, possibly architecture) visibly heals from cracking/failing v1 state through v_mid (cracks beginning to heal, regrowth) to v_endgame (vibrant + intact + possibly blooming, magical flame, no manual lighting needed).

**Strong cross-pillar alignment:**
- **Pillar 1 (Character Development):** Every environmental asset becomes a character-development record. Magic returns *through* villager-arc completion, which the wisp catalyzes. The world's visible state IS the player's relationship work, made permanent and shared.
- **Pillar 2 (Never Punishing):** Asset progression states are a SHOW mechanism, not a GATE. Players are not locked out of areas; v1 cracked lampposts are functional (villagers light them manually); healed v_endgame versions are aesthetic + lore payoff. No timer, no missable healing windows.
- **Pillar 3 (Approachability):** Diegetic damage telegraphs which infrastructure is grown (cracking, broken tines, hanging brackets) vs built (joinery, plank construction, hand-tool marks, paint-and-wear). Players read the world without instruction.

**Composes with just-landed (2026-05-16) architectural amendments:**
- **Amendment 3 (Exploration collectibles):** Grown-vs-built world state gives explorable world its narrative texture; collectibles can be grown-relic fragments or villager-crafted items, mapping to the same axis.
- **Amendment 4 (Crafting middle-ground three-rhythm):** Magic-return loop is the *narrative integration layer* that crafting hangs on — villagers teach crafting recipes as magic returns + their arcs progress; the crafting middle-ground produces both grown-style and built-style outputs depending on which villager taught it.
- **Amendment 1+2 (Faerie-house customization + tree-hole canvas):** Decoration sets carry the grown-vs-built signature; per-villager faerie-house sets reflect that villager's relationship to magic-age vs villager-craft register.

**Addresses Q5 survey attractors (MKTG-06 corpus closure 2026-05-16):**
- Art style / aesthetic (43%) — magic-return arc is the strongest single aesthetic-direction commitment in the project
- Exploration (43%) — world-state shifts give exploration its narrative weight
- Decoration (43%) — grown-vs-built register maps onto decoration system

**Setting-level commitments locked through this seed (if elevated):**
- Sterling's world had magic that grew functional infrastructure (lampposts canonically; interior heirlooms via Phase 3.1 commitment)
- Magic has receded; grown infrastructure is currently cracking/failing
- As magic returns (catalyzed by wisp's villager-arc work), grown infrastructure heals
- **Distribution rule (Phase 3.1):** mixed-mode at instance level with character/age-driven distribution. Grown furniture = older relics in homes of villagers old enough to have owned them since the magic-age (Cyrus canonically). Built furniture = post-magic-receding villager-craft (default modern register; younger villagers' households).
- **Architecture (buildings themselves):** OPEN — could be either, could be mixed within a single building. Daniel must author when Phase 3.2/3.3 (modular architecture) work begins.
- **Other infrastructure (fences, signs-as-standalone, doorways, bridges, wells):** OPEN — case-by-case at the moment each asset gets authored.

**Design DNA already locked (lampposts specifically):**
- Slim slender magically-grown wooden column, tapered wider at base
- Open-frame wooden-tine lantern with peaked cap, sits canonical upright on top of post
- Flame visible directly through tine gaps; container housing constant across versions; flame contents evolve (oil → witch-light → enchanted)
- Variable damage across instances within v1 (every village lamppost has DIFFERENT damage profile)
- Three damage philosophies explored during Phase 2.3: silhouette #1 (broken-bracket pragmatism, rejected), #2 (tree-trunk salvage, rejected), #3 (magically-grown cracking, **WON** — this is the v1 canon)
