## Hour-200 retention doc structural argument

The four engines (state-tagged dialogue / 3+ active narrative threads / seasonal recurrence / player-driven goals) compound with matchmaking-as-puzzle (D-VISION-04 meta layer) in a way that the original SUMMARY.md framework didn't yet capture (matchmaking was locked AFTER four-engine framework was authored). Each successful matchmaking creates:
- New state combinations for Engine 1 (paired-villager state × wisp-villager state × tonal-state × NG+ memory)
- New threads for Engine 2 (per-pairing relationship arc + cascading-thread ripples to adjacent villagers)
- New festival reactions for Engine 3 (paired villagers at year-over-year festivals; Beltane as matchmaking-arc climax; Samhain altar reflections)
- New player-driven goals for Engine 4 (matchmaking strategies across NG+ runs; village-as-canvas reflection of pair-roster; cat-with-Cyrus opens animal-rescue meta-anchor)

The structural argument: matchmaking provides a **combinatorial content multiplier** that none of the four engines have alone. Each match creates content for all four engines simultaneously. Slice (Phase 6) demonstrates this at C(4,2) = 6-pair scale; full game scales to C(12,2) = 66 pairs × 4 engines compounding × NG+ memory = a content-state space substantially larger than the engines alone would produce.
