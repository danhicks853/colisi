## 0:00 - 0:10 — Establish the cozy-magical register

**Visual:** Wisp drifts above a cloud barrier playing with vapor at dawn. Three cloud-ring cycles (~3 seconds; controls land organically). Wisp notices Sterling's faint glow piercing the cloud cover and dips through. **Sterling rises into view at golden hour** — small village nestled between ocean and mountains, smoke from morning hearths, silhouettes of villagers beginning their day.

**Audio:** Soft dawn-arrangement music; wisp's gentle ambient chime as it drifts through clouds; village ambience swells as the wisp descends.

**On-screen text:** None during this segment. Visual establishes the game's emotional register without words.

**Beat derived from:** Wow beat 1 (Sterling revealed at dawn).

**Persona-zero anchor:** *This is the moment a wishlist visitor decides to stay or close the tab.* Visual + audio register establishes "magical wonder + cozy village + golden hour aesthetic" in 10 seconds.

## 0:10 - 0:20 — The mechanic becomes legible

**Visual:** Wisp drifts inside the Elder's Abode. **Cyrus turns his head toward the wisp**, his unseeing eyes lifting; his weathered face softens with recognition. He gestures — first branching dialog choices appear briefly (player sees the choice surface). Cut to: a magic-tutoring minigame in progress (could be cooking with Sprinkles OR alchemy with Ari OR construction with Zhan — variant depending on which beat the demo features). The wisp pulses gently with each correct beat; the villager's craft visibly transforms (bread rises perfectly / potion glows the right color / wood joins without nails).

**Audio:** Cyrus's character-coded leitmotif begins; transitions into the featured villager's leitmotif during the minigame cut.

**On-screen text (brief):** *"You are the magic in their world."* — single line, ~2 seconds, optional. Daniel may decide this is unnecessary if visual is self-explanatory.

**Beats derived from:** Wow beat 2 (First contact with Cyrus) + Wow beat 5 (Magic-tutoring minigame in progress).

**Persona-zero anchor:** This is where the wishlist visitor learns *what they'll actually be doing* in the game. Spritesight + magic-tutoring layer becomes legible. Differentiates from "another farm game."

## 0:20 - 0:30 — Emotional pull + wishlist hook

**Visual:** Cut to: Ari at the Apothecary doorway. Cut to: Zhan working at Carv. Their eyes meet across the Town Center — moment of recognition. Wisp drifts in the foreground, gentle witness. The frame holds; Ari and Zhan move toward each other. As they approach their first kiss, **the screen text appears: "Thanks for playing — please wishlist."** Demo ends mid-kiss-approach.

**Audio:** Ari × Zhan duet leitmotif (composer arranges Ari's and Zhan's character themes intertwining); soft swell at the kiss approach; brief silence as wishlist text appears.

**On-screen text:** *"Thanks for playing — please wishlist."* (overlay during final 2 seconds).

**Beats derived from:** Wow beat 9 (Ari × Zhan compatibility surfacing) + Wow beat 10 (Cliffhanger first kiss).

**Persona-zero anchor:** The wishlist visitor sees the emotional climax + literal wishlist conversion text at the same beat. Direct pull. The unfinished kiss creates conversion pressure ("I want to see this complete = wishlist + buy full game").

## What the demo proves

5 design claims the 30-second demo makes explicit (or implicit through visual register):

- **No time pressure visible.** The wisp drifts at its own pace; no UI clocks, no countdown timers, no "hurry" framing. Pillar 2 honored on-screen.
- **Characters feel real.** Cyrus's recognition moment + Ari × Zhan eye-meeting establish that the cast has inner lives, not just stat sheets. Pillar 1 (character development) signal.
- **Matchmaking is the central puzzle.** The arc from "see Cyrus → catalyze villagers → witness Ari × Zhan chemistry" establishes that the player's role is bringing people together. D-VISION-04 surface.
- **The wisp is something new.** Player-as-wisp is a distinct premise from "player owns a farm" / "player builds an island" / "player is a magical chosen one." Cozy genre differentiation.
- **Cozy aesthetic in every frame.** Dawn lighting + village warmth + gentle character moments. No combat, no time pressure, no fail states. Pillar 2 + Pillar 3 visual signal.
