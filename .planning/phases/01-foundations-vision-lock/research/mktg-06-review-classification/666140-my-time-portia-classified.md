## CORPUS-LEVEL FINDING (Daniel takeaway 2026-05-10)

**"Reviews are based on a polished, engaging experience."**

After 2 verified games (Stardew + Portia), the dominant bounce pattern in this corpus is NOT the 5-category D-MKTG-02 anti-feature framework. It's:

1. **Engagement / "didn't click" / theme-vibe-feel mismatch** — Daniel's NS → other-engagement promotions across both games
2. **Bugs / crashes / production-quality** — dominant in Portia (43%), present in Stardew
3. **Time-pressure / difficulty / endgame** — present and meaningful but MINORITY signal

**Implication for Colisi positioning:** "Polished + engaging" is the baseline cozy players actually need. Anti-anti-feature differentiation (no time-pressure, no required-combat, no endgame-cliff) only matters AFTER the polish + engagement bar is cleared. This insight needs to surface in the eventual MARKET-EVIDENCE.md verdict.

**Implication for D-MKTG-02 framework:** The 5-category framework captures Colisi's specific architectural anti-clauses (well — empirical hits on TP/RC/EG confirmed). It does NOT capture the dominant bounce signal in the broader cozy market. This is not a framework failure — it was designed to test the anti-feature thesis, which is a Colisi-design-anchor question, not a "what bounces cozy players overall" question. But the verdict should acknowledge this scope.
