## Methodology notes

- **Spiritfarer's negative reviews have substantially higher signal-to-noise than Stardew's** — likely because Spiritfarer is a smaller game with more engaged-but-frustrated player base; Stardew's 700k+ review corpus contains lots of casual/joke reviews.
- **Dominant complaint pattern: TEDIUM / GRIND / RESOURCE-GATHERING-AS-BUSYWORK.** 14+ reviews cite some form of tedious gameplay loop or resource-gathering-padding-out-story.
- **Endgame complaints present** (R1, R4 explicitly; many tedium complaints intensify in late-game per R16).
- **Difficulty complaints around platforming/minigames** (R10, R11, R30) — the dexterity-heavy platformer mechanics tension persona-zero hits.
- **Accessibility complaints** (R9, R16) — flashing lights / no settings; cozy demographic includes disabled players; Colisi must learn from this (per UI-02 colorblind + UI-01 text scale + Pillar 3 approachability).
- **Marketed-as-cozy mismatch** (R26, R30) — players who came expecting cozy and got platformer/stress-coded experience; relevant to Colisi's positioning discipline.
- **R26 is notable for Colisi's design caveats**: "emotional black hole ... feel deeply guilty just for trying to help them" — this is exactly the failure mode that Cast Representation Principle's "no mature/trauma-coded themes" + Cross-pillar P1×P2 authoring discipline rule (3 checks: witness-not-fixer / no content-gating / bittersweet-held) is designed to prevent in Colisi.
- Persona-zero's Spiritfarer-bounce (per `PERSONA.md`) aligns with what reviewers cite: shallow gameplay + content-cliff late game.
