## NEW corpus category surfaced: "Value-for-gameplay"

Daniel introduced this category during A Short Hike verification. Distinct from:

- **Endgame (Pitfall 3 cliff):** "I invested 50+ hours and ran out of late-game meaningful content" — Spiritfarer / Stardew / Coral Island pattern
- **Engagement:** "Game didn't click for me / lost interest"
- **Value-for-gameplay:** "I paid $X expecting Y hours of meaningful content; this game is too short / shallow for the price"

**8 of 30 A Short Hike reviews carry value-for-gameplay complaint.** This is a separate complaint shape from the 5 D-MKTG-02 anti-feature categories and from engagement.

**Implication for Colisi:** Colisi's 30+hr scope target is the structural answer to value-for-gameplay complaints. NOT the same problem as endgame-cliff — the 30+hr scope addresses both (enough content to clear the value bar + enough late-game depth to avoid Pitfall 3). But these are TWO separate failure modes, not one.

## Methodology notes

- **A Short Hike is mis-shaped relative to the D-MKTG-02 framework**: complaints cluster on Pillar 3 (Approachability) UI-minimalism + value-for-gameplay pricing/length mismatch. Not the framework's 5 anti-feature categories.
- A Short Hike is short BY DESIGN (1-2 hour experience at low price); reviewers complaining about length didn't internalize the marketing. Same positioning-honesty pattern as Coffee Talk (markets as game, reads as VN) and Spiritfarer (markets as cozy, delivers platformer).
- **A Short Hike's intentionally-minimal UI (no map, no quest log, compass-only) creates real accessibility friction** — 7 reviews. Colisi's UI-01..10 + ONB-01..04 + new POLISH-N (2026-05-10) cluster is the structural response. Don't repeat A Short Hike's UI-minimalism in a 30+hr game.
- **R21 "Hope Punk twee garbage writing"** flagged as low-signal personal-preference noise; count but don't weight. Still relevant as cross-corpus calibration warning: cozy/wholesome tone CAN read as twee if execution lacks depth. Pairs with Hokko R28 "uncanny overly-positive NPCs." Colisi's D-VISION-14 bidirectional tonal arc + cross-quarter mourning tradition (Samhain) + character-arc bittersweet-held discipline are the structural answer.
- **R28 boat-race minigame difficulty** (similar to Cozy Grove R21 wheel-spin and Stardew R25 fair-wheel) — minigame difficulty CAN break cozy register. Colisi's magic-tutoring minigames (D-VISION-04) must not have hard fail states; warm/neutral/cool tonal range applies to mechanics too.
- A Short Hike is a useful **opposite-comparable** for Colisi: short adventure vs Colisi's 30+hr full game. Different problem shape; different anti-feature failure mode (value-for-gameplay vs endgame-cliff). The hour-200+ design intent (per `23-why-play-at-hour-200.md`) addresses both.
