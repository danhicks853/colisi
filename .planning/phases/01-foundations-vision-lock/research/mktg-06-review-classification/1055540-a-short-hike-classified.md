## Methodology notes

- A Short Hike's "endgame" complaint is inverted from Stardew/Spiritfarer pattern: it's "**too short**" rather than "ran out of late-game content." Different shape of the same anti-feature category (content-completion mismatch with expectation).
- Most common complaint: **no map / poor orientation guidance / collectathon-tedium** (7+ reviews) — relevant to Pillar 3 (Approachability) + NARR-07 (telegraph puzzle solutions) — A Short Hike's intentionally-minimal UI creates Approachability friction for some players.
- **Difficulty in minigames** (R28 boat race) similar to Cozy Grove's R21 — minigame difficulty can break cozy register.
- A Short Hike is a useful **opposite-comparable** for Colisi: short adventure vs Colisi's 30+hr full game; Colisi's hour-200+ design intent (per `23-why-play-at-hour-200.md`) is the inverse problem.
