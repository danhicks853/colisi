## Methodology notes

- **Coral Island's signal-to-noise is dominated by TECHNICAL ISSUES (bugs, crashes, performance, multiplayer-broken).** Players are clearly engaged but frustrated by execution.
- **Time-pressure complaints surface differently:** less about deliberate time-pressure mechanics; more about *losing day-progress to crashes* (R10, R13). This indirectly speaks to cozy promise — Coral Island players want to NOT lose their day, but bugs make them.
- **Endgame complaints intensify in Year 2** (R4, R21) — comparable cozy life sim hits content cliff at predictable point. Direct support for Pitfall 3 (endgame content cliff is one of the four killers).
- **LGBT representation controversy** (R1, R6, R22, R30) — relevant to Colisi's Cast Representation Principle anti-clauses. Coral Island's mandated-flag approach backfired; Colisi's normalized-without-ceremony approach is structurally different. Worth noting as anti-example for what NOT to do.
- **Multiplayer-design complaints** are not "required multiplayer" (Coral Island is solo-playable) but rather multiplayer being broken when attempted. Different category than persona-zero's Palia complaint (required multiplayer).
- **Stardew-comparison complaints** (R19, R23) — Coral Island gets compared unfavorably to Stardew. Important Colisi positioning lesson: not-derivative-of-Stardew matters for differentiation.
