- **Coffee Talk validates the locked Out-of-Scope anti-clause against VN-format** (per CLAUDE.md cozy promise: "Visual-novel format — Persona quit Coffee Talk for this. We are 3D and embodied.").

Market the actual experience honestly — 3D embodied character work with magic-tutoring minigames and matchmaking puzzles. The Coffee Talk bounce pattern is a positioning-honesty cautionary tale, not just a format-format cautionary tale.
