- R12 is a long, well-reasoned complaint specifically about energy/stamina mechanics — Stardew's stamina system is the exact anti-clause locked in cozy promise.
- R18 is highly relevant to Colisi's positioning: explicitly says "for busy overworked adults who just wanna chill ... don't play this. Go for an actually cozy and relaxing option." This is a wishlist-target persona articulating they want Colisi-shaped products.
- Daniel-verification will likely refine categories (e.g., R7 might be re-tagged "other (no-run-mechanic)" rather than time-pressure; R25 might be re-tagged differently).
