## CRITICAL: anti-clause gap surfaced (R24)

**Daniel annotation on R24** *(waiting for copper to melt in realtime is cringe)*:

> "this is a real concern: we have forbade punishing decrement mechanics but have not addressed punishing forward-time timing."

**The gap:** Pillar 2 / Cozy Promise currently prohibits *in-game* time-pressure (day clocks, missable windows, stamina, tool durability, bedtime forcing) and decrement mechanics. **It does NOT explicitly prohibit real-time forward-timer gating** — mechanics that require the player to wait X real-world minutes/hours for a resource or process to complete (e.g., "wait 4 hours for copper to melt"; Dreamlight Valley's "30 min to 4 hours for one item" per R16 same complaint shape).

**Why this matters:**
- Real-time forward-timer gating IS punishing — it forces wait-or-come-back-later friction
- It's mobile-game logic (energy-gated retention) that breaks immersion in cozy life sim
- It compounds with daily-time-gates (already-forbidden) but is a distinct mechanism
- Persona-zero's cozy-promise expectation ("play as much/little as I want") is violated by both

**Proposed anti-clause extension (queued for cross-verification with Dreamlight Valley R16):**

> "No real-time forward-timer gating of progression. Resources and crafted items must be available within a single play session; players cannot be forced to wait real-world hours for content to unlock."

This needs Daniel's explicit confirmation + cross-verification with Dreamlight Valley (which has the strongest signal for this anti-feature). Architectural commit deferred pending Dreamlight verification.
