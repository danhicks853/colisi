## R12
**ID:** 224942795 | **Date:** 2026-05-07 | **Helpful:** 1 | **Playtime:** 5.0h

> Let me preface this by saying, it started out life as what I would consider a very good game for what it was. It did what it was meant to probably the best out of every cozy game I've tried. I still play, still mostly run it on the original proprietary launcher I got from the website. I've been playing almost since launch.

Pros
It still is, at ground level, a very good game. The movement is smooth, the mechanics are intuitive, the graphics are beautiful.
Everything you collect can be used for something, and it's very easy to start and maintain a profitable vegetable garden.
You will quickly end up with every fruit and vegetable you can grow being higher quality than base (hereafter referred to as Star Quality or SQ for convenience). This in turn means that meals you cook for yourself have a higher chance of turning out SQ as well, even thought the tool tips claim it has no effect.
Fishing and hunting are fun, mostly. It has gotten less fun as of late, but we will get there.
Addition of animal farming has been a very nice and quite enjoyable touch.
Elderwoods, despite being somewhat confusing to navigate initially, has become a favorite map, and I am looking forward to the release of The Royal Highlands expansion.
The NPC friendship/romance model is quite well done in my opinion. Chatting with the characters is enjoyable, and the dateable characters are all loveable in their own ways. Talking to them almost always results in new information about the one you're talking to or one of the other characters, and they all know each other so far. You can maintain two romantic relationships at any one time. I haven't done any in-depth testing yet to see if chosen relationships are permanent, but given how the rest of the game works progression wise I would err on the side of caution.

Cons
Premium currency, and amounts needed for new cash shop items.
This wasn't a huge deal initially, Pal Coins can be bought for a theoretically reasonable price and new outfits were originally set more or less a maximum of roughly 2,550.
This didn't take long to change however, initially to 2,570 and now nearly all new outfits are 3,400. It's absolutely absurd.
This means that the pricing for Pal Coins is also directly and aggressively absurd to the point of being predatory.
The loyalty club feels like a blatant and intentional scam.
You will spend OVER 3 MONTHS earning enough Pal Coins to buy almost any of the newer outfits.
Given the Pal Coin prize from the recent contest in preparation for the new expansion, I have a sneaking suspicion that whatever new cosmetic they have cooked up is going to be over the 5,000 Pal Coin mark.
This is my biggest gripe, but it is by no means the only one.

Farming and how food quality works.
You will quickly find yourself in the position of having all SQ garden produce, but few to zero SQ wild gatherable cooking supplies. They fixed the problem of having not enough SQ wild garlic and spice sprouts, but everything else barely grows as SQ (in fact it's even more rare than it was originally). SQ sweet leaf etc used to be easy to come by, but now you can spend hours hunting it and only come up with 2-3.
Hunting animals is so much worse.
Originally the drop rates were good, you could reliably expect roughly 1-2 SQ meat drops for every 4-6 animals taken. That number has not only been reduced, it has been so harshly throttled that you will only see at BEST 1 SQ meat drop for every 10 animals taken, and this isn't accounting for animals that drop no meat at all.
On to the animals themselves: Chappa are annoying. Mujinn are outright cancerous. Forget bagging the mythic versions of either of these monstrosities, even in a group most of the time. A dishonorable mention goes to Sernuk which in the past couple months seem to have developed the ability for no good reason to dodge shots right as those shots reach them. You can be right on target, your arrow will be right there, then boom, rotten thing jukes to the side and you miss because this stupid goat magically somehow grew eyes in the back of its head to see it. Anyone who has played in Bahari Bay for more than five minutes knows exactly what I'm talking about. Combine that with the absurd decision to destroy the drop rates and thus the enjoyability of hunting, and the game becomes rather less enticing.

To Note:
Fishing doesn't really suffer from this, it still works exactly the same as it did on launch, the problems with that are Elderwood specific. We will get there.

This hunting issue really becomes pronounced however when it comes to cooking. I haven't quite worked out if you can mix food, item quality wise, as I have yet to run out of SQ ingredients, meat and otherwise.
I am getting closer though.
See, when you open the cooking menu, you choose which quality items to use, and it then pulls from your storage to complete the recipe. I am not sure if this is a UI fail or if this is something else, but if you leave it on base quality, Stars don't show up. If you switch the menu to use SQ items however, base quality doesn't show up. The implication is that you can't mix one tier with the other tier. This would be fine, except for the entirely artificially created meat supply problem.

There are other huntable animal issues as well, again specific to the Elderwood. We will get there.

The cooking mechanic does have one other minor issue, in that your game can be running perfectly fine the entire time, but once you start the cooking process your game can be randomly and unpredictably be hit by sudden latency issues. Not registering cuts, not playing the sounds correctly for accurately performed actions, the visual queues not appearing correctly, etc. Minor. They are a massive problem though if everything decides to malfunction all at once, which I have had happen on occasion. I attribute this more to network connection quality than anything usually, although it is brow raising that it almost never happens for other activities.

Here we get to Elderwoods.

Mostly minor issues, the most notable on that front being no more tool upgrades. You still have Palium tools, which are great for Kilima and Bahari, but in some cases just won't cut it in Elderwoods.
Fishing is the best example for this, there are "artifacts" you can build and upgrade, but so far there don't seem to be any to help with actually hooking and reeling in fish. No pole upgrades from Palium also means that Elderwood fish are as feisty as Kilima fish are for newbies with the makeshift rod.

Arrows and bug bombs however are a major issue, the fine arrows won't cut it for some of the huntables in Elderwoods, don't do enough damage, there are also a special ammo type using the new resource (Echo) from the Elderwoods, and Elderwoods arrows only do about as much damage as a makeshift arrow. Apart from Mujinn, every huntable in the Elderwood has an absurd HP pool comparatively. You will waste more arrows and more effort than most of these obnoxious little monsters are worth to bag. There is not a single non-annoying thing to hunt here. That said, Shmoles are irritating but at least they don't run and can be killed with relatively low issues. Ogupuu however. My GOD. Mujinn are cancerous. Ogupuu however are like cancer that has chlamydia that has herpes that has the flu. They run, way faster than they need to, like Chapas they actually disappear (into the water) so unlike Mujinn there's no way to force the issue, and as if that wasn't enough they vomit movement imparing goo at you and prevent you from effectively chasing them. This is unnecessary and feels even more mean spirited then Mujinn feel. It's almost impossible to do this even with a team. It's even worse when parts from these little ♥♥♥♥ bags are part of event bundles needed for ingame rewards.

There is more but I'm running out of space. All this to say, this game has grown much worse over time and I can't recommend it. Choose something else that respects your time and your wallet.
