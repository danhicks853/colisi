# Anti-Positioning

## Vs. Stardew Valley

> **PRE-FILLED CANDIDATE — review, edit voice, reject anchor drift, sign off.** Stardew is *the* most load-bearing comparable: it's persona-zero (Dawn)'s #1 game at 1000+ hours, and Colisi's persona-gap was shaped against it specifically (`research/PERSONA.md` lines 72–74, `research/SUMMARY.md`).

**Differentiator:** Colisi is a 3D embodied life sim with a non-human player avatar (wisp) whose agency is catalytic-not-direct, with matchmaking-via-environmental-manipulation as its central puzzle layer — none of which Stardew shares.

**We share:**
- Small village setting with persistent named NPCs
- Cozy tonal register (warm, low-stakes, relationship-driven)
- Crafting systems that produce gift-able items with NPC-relationship effects
- A relationship core that is the deepest part of the game *(per `PERSONA.md` line 73: "Interacting with the characters is what kept me coming back" — the persona-zero retention proof)*

**We diverge:**
- **Never punishing.** Stardew has day clocks, missable seasonal events, energy depletion, tool-durability-adjacent friction, and farm-decay if you neglect it — *all of which are forbidden in Colisi by Pillar 2 anti-clauses.* Per `PERSONA.md` line 74, Stardew's time-pressure is Dawn's #1 *hate* despite her 1000+ hours; Colisi keeps the relationship core that worked, drops the time pressure that didn't.
- **Catalytic, not direct, player agency.** In Stardew the player IS the farmer-protagonist with full direct agency over actions and outcomes. In Colisi the wisp catalyzes villagers into agency over themselves (D-VISION-02 latent magic surfacing through relationship; D-VISION-03 sprite gives, never accumulates). Different fantasy: Stardew = "I am a farmer building my life"; Colisi = "I am a quiet presence helping others build theirs."
- **Matchmaking-via-environmental-manipulation as the central puzzle layer.** Stardew has no equivalent — relationships in Stardew progress via gift-giving + heart-event triggers. Colisi's central puzzle (D-VISION-04 meta layer) is the wisp engineering villager-to-villager connection through environmental nudges. Different verb entirely.
- **BG3-style branching dialog and authored arcs.** Stardew's dialogue is heart-tier-gated string variants; Colisi's is state-flag-loose branching with multi-stage NPC arcs (NPC-04, NARR-01) — the BG3 × Sims dream cross per D-VISION-06.
- **Per-villager experiential replay system** (NARR-06 reframe, 2026-05-10): Colisi treats character history as something the player can *witness as a montage*, not just read in a journal. Stardew has no equivalent.
- **3D embodied vs 2D pixel-art top-down.** Different presentation register; informs Pillar 3 hardware-accessibility tradeoffs (Stardew runs on anything; Colisi must engineer for integrated GPUs per DIST-06).
- **Not a farming sim.** Farming as a daily-cycle resource-management loop is *not* a Colisi mechanic — no crop cycles, no daily watering, no season-locked crops. Wild gathering and cozy-shape pet-care candidates (per `persona-zero-emergent-feedback.md` 2026-05-10) may exist; farming-as-time-management does not.

**We do NOT claim to be:**
- We do NOT claim to be a "Stardew Killer." *(Per PHILOSOPHY.md §III communication guardrails — comparable-killer framings are forbidden marketing traps regardless of how confident the team is in the persona-gap pivot.)*
- We do NOT claim to be "Stardew but X" — neither "Stardew but in 3D" nor "Stardew but with magic" nor "Stardew but matchmaking." Colisi is a different game shape with a different core fantasy. Stardew's relationship core inspired the persona-gap research; Colisi is built *for* the demographic Stardew partially serves but doesn't fully retain.
- Colisi is **not a cozy farming sim.** It's a cozy life sim with magic-tutoring and matchmaking puzzle layers as its central activities. Genre framing matters at Steam-page time (Phase 8); the genre tag will reflect this.

## Vs. Baldur's Gate 3

> **PRE-FILLED CANDIDATE.** BG3 is half of the locked dream cross (D-VISION-06). Persona-zero loved BG3's *"It makes me feel like I'm less alone"* (PERSONA.md line 37) but bounced on its difficulty-curve ramp at endgame (PERSONA.md line 38).

**Differentiator:** Colisi takes BG3's branching dialog + companion-relationship depth and applies them to a non-combat, never-punishing cozy register — preserving the *"less alone"* emotional core while removing every difficulty wall and combat axis.

**We share:**
- Branching authored dialog with persistent state-flag consequences (NPC-05, NARR-01)
- Multi-stage companion arcs that evolve through accompaniment (NPC-04, COMP-01..03)
- Decisions that meaningfully impact world-state and NPC behavior (NARR-01, NARR-03)
- The *"less alone"* emotional quality persona-zero explicitly cited (PERSONA.md line 37)

**We diverge:**
- **No combat of any kind.** BG3 is a turn-based combat RPG; Colisi forbids all interactive player combat per Pillar 2 anti-clause 4. Per PERSONA.md line 38, BG3's difficulty-curve ramp at endgame is Dawn's primary BG3 frustration (*"I don't feel like I've built up the skills to finish the game"*) — Colisi removes the entire combat axis, not just dampens it.
- **No difficulty curve / no skill walls.** BG3's RPG escalation toward endgame is the persona-gap demographic's bounce reason (also true for Fallout, Hogwarts Legacy, Spiritfarer per PERSONA.md). Colisi has Pillar 2's never-punishing constraint — no skill-check failures gate progress.
- **Catalytic-not-direct agency.** BG3 has a player-character protagonist with full party-leader agency. Colisi has the wisp catalyzing villagers' agency through environmental manipulation (D-VISION-02..03).
- **Cozy tonal register.** BG3 has high-fantasy adventure with mature themes (sex, violence, death-as-stake). Colisi is cozy with no mature-thematic content.
- **No party-based mechanics.** BG3's party-of-companions in combat is core; Colisi's wisp moves solo, with companion accompaniment as relationship progression (COMP-01..03), not for combat.

**We do NOT claim to be:**
- We do NOT claim to be a "BG3 Killer" or "BG3 but cozy" — comparable-killer framings forbidden per PHILOSOPHY.md §III.
- Colisi is **not a combat RPG**, full stop — per Pillar 2 anti-clause 4 and the OOS-list amendment from "required combat" to "interactive player combat mechanics of any kind" (SCOPE.md 2026-05-10).
- Colisi takes *one half* of the dream cross from BG3 (branching dialog + companion arc); we are not "BG3-without-combat" — Colisi has its own central puzzle layer (matchmaking-via-environmental-manipulation) that BG3 does not have.

## Vs. The Sims

> **PRE-FILLED CANDIDATE.** The Sims is the other half of the locked dream cross (D-VISION-06). Persona-zero loved *"having control, building a life, seeing how I can affect the happiness of others with my choices"* (PERSONA.md line 45) but bounced on the *"then what?"* endgame problem (PERSONA.md line 46).

**Differentiator:** Colisi takes Sims-like world-manipulation agency and *inverts the locus of control* — the player catalyzes villagers' own agency rather than directly controlling them, and structurally solves the *"then what?"* problem through evolving relationships that don't expire.

**We share:**
- World-manipulation as a player verb (the wisp engineering meet-cutes echoes Sims' player-as-life-architect role)
- Relationship-driven retention engine (per PERSONA.md line 45 — affecting villagers' lives via choices is the loved-aspect)
- Persistent characters whose lives evolve over time
- Small-stakes domestic narrative as content material

**We diverge:**
- **Catalytic-not-direct agency.** Sims gives the player full direct control over Sims (move them, employ them, dress them, marry them, kill them). Colisi gives the wisp *catalytic-but-not-direct* agency over villagers (D-VISION-02..03 — the wisp nudges environment to spark villager-driven outcomes; villagers retain their own narrative agency). Different fantasy: Sims = *"I am a god of small lives"*; Colisi = *"I am a quiet presence helping others find their own path."*
- **Solves the *"then what?"* endgame problem** that persona-zero explicitly cited (PERSONA.md line 46). Sims' content runs out when a Sim dies + boredom hits; Colisi is built around evolving relationships that don't end (NARR-05 game continues meaningfully after main story; the four-engine retention strategy from `research/SUMMARY.md`; NARR-06 per-villager replay system).
- **Embodied 3D world with one player avatar.** Sims is a top-down household-management simulator with multi-Sim micromanagement; Colisi is a 3D embodied world with a single wisp the player navigates as.
- **Branching authored dialog.** Sims has voiced-but-incoherent Simlish + behavioral interactions; Colisi has BG3-style branching authored dialog (D-VISION-06 dream cross) with state-flag consequences (NPC-05, NARR-01).
- **No life-stage death cycle.** Sims' core loop ends with Sim death and resets via children; Colisi has no death-as-content-cap (NARR-04 no forced-tragic; villagers' lives continue indefinitely; D-VISION-07 romance between villagers, not player-mediated).
- **Single-player-only.** Sims has online + co-op layers; Colisi is single-player-core (per OOS list — persona-zero quit Palia for required multiplayer).

**We do NOT claim to be:**
- We do NOT claim to be a "Sims Killer" or "Sims but X" — comparable-killer framings forbidden per PHILOSOPHY.md §III.
- Colisi is **not a life-management simulator** — the fantasy is helping-not-controlling, surfacing-not-architecting.
- Colisi takes *the world-manipulation agency half* of the dream cross from Sims (D-VISION-06); we are not "Sims-with-magic" — Colisi has its own central puzzle layer that Sims does not have.

## Vs. Spiritfarer

> **PRE-FILLED CANDIDATE.** Persona-zero loved Spiritfarer's *"going out and collecting things with story attached, crafting"* (PERSONA.md line 65) but bounced on the difficulty-curve ramp (PERSONA.md line 66) — same pattern as BG3, Fallout, and Hogwarts Legacy.

**Differentiator:** Colisi shares Spiritfarer's cozy register and story-tied-collection appeal but rejects both the grief-driven emotional contract *and* the difficulty-curve ramp — the wisp's arc is finding belonging that lasts, not learning to let go.

**We share:**
- Cozy tonal register
- Crafting tied to character story (PERSONA.md line 65 — Dawn's loved-aspect)
- Going out and collecting things *with story attached* (PERSONA.md line 65 — explicit loved-aspect)
- Relationship depth as central gameplay
- Hand-crafted painterly aesthetic adjacency

**We diverge:**
- **No grief as primary emotional register.** Spiritfarer's central arc is letting go / saying goodbye to friends who depart by Stygian boat. Colisi's central arc is forming and deepening relationships that *don't end* (NARR-04 no forced-tragic, NARR-05 game continues meaningfully after main story, D-VISION-07 villager arcs continue indefinitely). Different emotional contract.
- **No difficulty-curve ramp.** Per PERSONA.md line 66, Dawn's Spiritfarer bounce was difficulty escalation. Colisi has Pillar 2's never-punishing constraint — no skill walls, no escalation pressure.
- **No required loss-of-companion fail-state.** COMP-04 explicitly forbids *"companion has died / left forever"* fail-states; Spiritfarer's narrative IS those moments by design.
- **Branching dialog and agency-over-village-state.** Spiritfarer is more authored-narrative-on-rails; Colisi is state-flag-loose branching with player-driven world-state (NARR-01..05).
- **The wisp's belonging arc, not departure-acceptance arc.** Spiritfarer is *"you ferry souls toward goodbye"*; Colisi is *"you find belonging through bonds that grow."* The wisp's loneliness-into-belonging arc (per Core Fantasy paragraph) is structurally inverted from Spiritfarer's belonging-into-grief arc.

**We do NOT claim to be:**
- We do NOT claim to be a "Spiritfarer Killer" or "Spiritfarer but happier" — comparable-killer framings forbidden per PHILOSOPHY.md §III.
- Colisi is **not a cozy game about death or grief.** We respect Spiritfarer's craft and emotional power; we are not its register and we are not building toward its arc.

## Vs. AI-generated cozy games

> **PRE-FILLED CANDIDATE — load-bearing positioning section.** This section is a structural commitment, not a comparison to a single game — it positions Colisi against the *category* of AI-generated cozy content reaching Steam. Per PHILOSOPHY.md §V and AI-DISCLOSURE.md, Colisi's AI policy is durable and predates any specific competitor's AI use.

**Differentiator:** Colisi is a human-crafted cozy life sim with a public AI use disclosure committing to no AI-generated assets, dialogue, names, or narrative content. AI-generated cozy games occupy adjacent shelf-space at the genre tag level but are built on a fundamentally different production posture — one Colisi explicitly declines.

**We share:**
- Genre adjacency at the Steam-tag level (cozy, life sim, magical-realism)
- Visual style ambitions targeting painterly / cozy aesthetics
- Some target-demographic overlap (cozy players browsing for new content)

**We diverge:**
- **All authorial content is human-generated.** Per `AI-DISCLOSURE.md` and `PHILOSOPHY.md` §V, Colisi commits explicitly to no AI-generated art, music, dialogue, item descriptions, character/NPC/place/mechanic names, devlog content, Steam page copy, marketing tweets, or community posts. This commitment is enforced internally by Daniel's directive *and* by Claude's role in this project (per CLAUDE.md, Claude refuses to generate prohibited content even when asked).
- **AI is used for code-assist, planning, and brainstorming only** — not for shipped player-facing content. The AI use is disclosed publicly in `AI-DISCLOSURE.md`.
- **Steam reputation risk is a documented design concern.** Per `research/SUMMARY.md` and the OOS list (*"AI-generated content for shipped product (art, music, dialogue, voice) — Steam reputation risk, copyright provenance unsettled, persona's demographic values human craft"*), AI-generated content carries Steam-rejection risk and player-trust risk.
- **The deep-cozy demographic values human craft.** Persona-zero's love-list explicitly includes *"crafting"* (5+ explicit mentions in PERSONA.md) and storyline depth — the categories where AI-generated content most visibly fails. Colisi is built for the demographic that is *most likely* to detect and reject AI-generated cozy content.
- **Hand-authored from day one.** Every villager dialogue line, every item description, every place name, every magic-system codification is Daniel-authored or human-collaborator-authored. No AI-generated content exists in the shipped product.

**We do NOT claim to be:**
- We do NOT claim to be *"better than AI-generated games"* — that's a marketing trap and an unstable comparison; we let the craft speak for itself.
- We do NOT participate in the AI-content arms race. The project's AI policy predates any specific competitor's AI use and is durable across the 4-6 year project horizon.
- We do NOT use AI in the shipped product. This is a commitment, not a goal.
