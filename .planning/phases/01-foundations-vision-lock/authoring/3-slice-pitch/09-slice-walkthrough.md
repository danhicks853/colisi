# Slice Scenario Sketch — Colisi

## Slice premise

You, a mysterious magical energy known as a wisp, discover Sterling, a small village nestled between the ocean and the mountains. After brief observation of its denizens, you happen across Cyrus, an ancient town elder with the ability to feel your presence and interact with you. You are then tasked with helping the townsfolk realize their own potential, and the potential of those around them.

---

## Setup

The wisp drifts aimlessly above a cloud barrier, playing with vapor and currents — mostly out of boredom. It has been like this for some time. The wisp is above the cloud cover and cannot yet see what lies below. The cloud-ring tutorial unfolds in this sky environment; only at tutorial's end does the wisp dip beneath the clouds and see Sterling for the first time, the village's faint glow rising to meet it as dawn breaks.

### Day 1 — Dawn

**Scene 1: Cloud-ring tutorial** (0:00-0:05) — Above the cloud barrier. Wisp drifts, playing with vapor. Player learns WASD/camera/interact via 3 cloud-ring cycles (wisp creates ring, flies through; per first-5-min cell of player journey). **Pillar showcase:** Pillar 2 (gentle wisp feedback + camera reset if rings missed — no fail state), Pillar 3 (controls land organically, "wisp entertains itself"). **Systems:** ONB-01..04, CTRL-01..03.

**Scene 2: Sterling revealed** (0:05) — Wisp notices Sterling's faint glow piercing the cloud cover. Wisp dips through the cloud barrier. Sterling appears below at dawn — golden light, the village nestled between ocean and mountains. **Wow-beat candidate** for trailer/capsule. **Pillar showcase:** Pillar 1 (the village pulls the wisp — first emotional draw). **Systems:** WORLD-01..02.

**Scene 3: Wisp enters Sterling** (0:05-0:08) — Wisp drifts into Town Center. Dawn light, villagers beginning their morning routines. None see the wisp. **Pillar showcase:** Pillar 1 (atmospheric character context). **Systems:** WORLD-01..02, NPC-02 (daily schedules visible).

**Scene 4: Observe Sprinkles at the Bakery** (~0:08) — Wisp drifts past Sprinkles Amazing Bakery of Unimaginably delicious treats. Sprinkles bakes with playful energy, humming. The wisp's POI-narration introduces her warmth + character context. Sprinkles cannot see the wisp. **Pillar showcase:** Pillar 1 (character development via observation). **Systems:** NPC-01..02, NPC-05 (state-tagged dialogue not yet triggerable pre-spritesight).

**Scene 5: Observe Ari at the Apothecary** (~0:10) — Wisp drifts past Apothecary. Ari brews quietly, anti-social, eccentric. Wisp's POI-narration introduces her eccentric craft + solitude. Ari cannot see the wisp. **Pillar showcase:** Pillar 1. **Systems:** NPC-01..02.

**Scene 6: Observe Zhan at Carv** (~0:12) — Wisp drifts past Carv. Zhan works wood-craft quietly, shy, demure. Wisp's POI-narration introduces his patience + quiet competence. Zhan cannot see the wisp. **Pillar showcase:** Pillar 1. **Systems:** NPC-01..02.

**Scene 7: First contact with Cyrus** (0:15-0:25) — Wisp drifts toward the Elder's Abode. Cyrus, ancient and blind, senses the wisp's presence via psychic knowledge. **First branching dialog opens** (per NPC-05 + NARR-01). Cyrus greets the wisp; first psychic-knowledge magic-tutoring minigame fires (non-visual mechanic per Cyrus's blindness — possibly memory, pattern, audio, or felt-presence-based). Cyrus introduces the **spritesight gift** mechanic: he can teach other villagers to perceive the wisp, but they must come to him. **Pillar showcase:** all 3 pillars (Pillar 1 first character interaction; Pillar 2 no fail state in psychic minigame; Pillar 3 spritesight introduced gently and diegetically). **Systems:** NPC-01..05, NARR-01..02, COMP-02, D-VISION-12 (spritesight introduced).

### Day 1 — Mid-morning to evening (player chooses spritesight gift order)

**Scene 8: First spritesight gift** (0:25-0:35) — Player chooses which villager to spritesight first (Sprinkles / Ari / Zhan; player-driven order per D-VISION-13 replayability). Wisp performs covert guidance to draw the chosen villager to the Elder's Abode. **Covert-guidance example (Sprinkles, if she's first):** Sprinkles and Ari are friends. Ari occasionally wanders near the Elder's Abode. Wisp ripens a fruit Sprinkles previously mentioned. Ari notices the fruit, mentions it to Sprinkles, Sprinkles retrieves the fruit, wisp alerts Cyrus when Sprinkles is near. Cyrus bestows spritesight. First dialog with that villager opens; first corresponding magic-tutoring minigame fires (cooking, alchemy, or construction depending on player order). **Pillar showcase:** all 3 pillars. **Systems:** NPC-01..05, NARR-01, COMP-01..04, CRAFT-01..05, D-VISION-12 (spritesight bestowal), D-VISION-08 (sprite-premise reinterpretation of CRAFT — wisp catalyzes, villager crafts).

**Scene 9: Wisp rests at tree hollow** (~0:35-0:37) — End of day 1. Wisp returns to its starting sanctum (tree hollow). Few-trigger model: rest advances calendar to next morning. **Pillar showcase:** Pillar 2 (rest is player-initiated, no forced advancement). **Systems:** SAVE-01..04, D-TIME-01..05 (rest trigger).

### Day 2

**Scene 10: Second spritesight gift** (0:37-0:47) — Day 2 dawns. Wisp drifts back into Sterling. Player chooses second villager for spritesight; another covert-guidance chain to bring them to Cyrus; another spritesight bestowal; another magic-tutoring minigame fires. **Pillar showcase:** all 3 pillars; cascading thread surfaces (the first sighted villager's mention of the wisp in casual village chatter sets up the second sighted villager's curiosity). **Systems:** NPC-01..05, NARR-01..03 (multi-thread narrative threads activating).

**Scene 11: Day 2 ambient exploration** (0:47-0:50) — Player drifts through Sterling. **D-VISION-10 Layer 2 upgrade slot may fire** (e.g., first villager's arc reaches a milestone; villagers initiate a shared-space transformation in honor of the wisp; player picks from 2-4 authored alternatives). Cat may appear during this beat if not yet discovered. **Pillar showcase:** Pillar 1 (cascading thread + earned aesthetic). **Systems:** D-VISION-10 Layer 2 upgrade slot.

**Scene 12: Day 2 rest** (~0:50) — Player rests at tree hollow. Day advances to day 3.

### Day 3

**Scene 13: Third spritesight gift** (0:50-0:55) — Final villager gets spritesight via final covert-guidance chain; final magic-tutoring minigame available (now player has experienced 3 of 4 minigames; psychic was scene 7). **Systems:** All NPC + NARR + COMP + CRAFT minus the few that don't fire in slice.

**Scene 14: Ari × Zhan compatibility surfaces** (0:55-0:58) — Cascading thread surfaces: Ari and Zhan have noticed each other now that both are sighted; their crafts (alchemy + magical construction) hint at collaborative possibility (Ari's potions for Zhan's nervous hands; Zhan's woodwork for Ari's hedge). Cyrus may hint at the compatibility, OR the wisp observes them seeing each other and feels the spark. **Player decides to attempt the matchmaking.** Per D-VISION-13, telegraphed as high-compatibility (no "are you sure?" warning); player can also attempt low-compat alternatives but they get telegraphed warnings ("Sprinkles seems pretty high octane, and Zhan is, well, Zhan" / etc.). **Pillar showcase:** all 3 pillars (Pillar 1 emergent matchmaking layer; Pillar 2 telegraphed not hidden; Pillar 3 player agency clear). **Systems:** NPC-09 (gossip-as-match-info), NARR-01..05, COMP-01..04, D-VISION-13.

**Scene 15: D-VISION-10 Layer 2 second upgrade slot** (0:58) — Second village-as-canvas upgrade slot fires (villager-arc milestone, e.g., Ari's confidence breakthrough leading to her approaching Zhan); villagers offer the wisp a shared-space transformation choice. **Possible faerie house unlock** (D-VISION-10 Layer 3) if one of the 4 villager arcs reaches full completion within slice scope — likely Ari's or Zhan's at matchmaking-readiness. **Systems:** D-VISION-10 Layer 2 + Layer 3.

**Scene 16: Cliffhanger — Ari × Zhan first-kiss approach** (0:59-1:00+) — Meet-cute beat lands. Ari and Zhan move to share their first kiss. **"Thanks for playing — please wishlist" overlay appears** as the slice ends. The kiss is NOT seen; the cliffhanger is structural — direct conversion pressure from emotional climax + wishlist message at the same beat. **Pillar showcase:** all 3 pillars + slice's primary wishlist conversion mechanism. **Systems:** NARR-04 (multiple satisfying endings; this kiss is one possible outcome of the chosen matchmaking attempt).

## Slice scope notes

### IN slice scope (Phase 6 must produce)

- 4 villagers fully authored (Sprinkles, Cyrus, Ari, Zhan): 3-stage arc state machines per NPC-04; multi-axis relationships per NPC-03; state-tagged dialogue per NPC-05
- Wisp character: nameless, genderless, narration voice, drift movement, cloud-ring tutorial behaviors
- Stray black cat: discoverable, player-namable, arc NOT completing in slice
- 7 locations: Sterling (village), Town Center, The Elder's Abode, Sprinkles Amazing Bakery of Unimaginably delicious treats, Apothecary, Carv, tree hollow
- Spritesight mechanic (D-VISION-12): pre-sighted observation + spritesight gift ceremony + post-sighted direct dialog
- All 4 magic-tutoring minigames (psychic / cooking / alchemy / construction)
- Matchmaking system: Ari × Zhan high-compat pair (full meet-cute through first-kiss cliffhanger); 3 low-compat alternatives with telegraph + minimal-outcome content (Sprinkles × Zhan, Sprinkles × Ari, Anyone × Cyrus)
- D-VISION-10 Layer 2 village-as-canvas: 2 upgrade slots, each with 2-4 authored alternatives (~4-8 aesthetic variations total)
- D-VISION-10 Layer 3 faerie house: 1 design (whichever single villager arc completes in slice, if any) — likely Ari's or Zhan's at matchmaking-readiness
- Few-trigger model: rest at tree hollow as sole day-advancement trigger in slice (no festivals in slice scope); ~3 day-rollovers
- Character-coded leitmotifs (AUDIO-01): 4 leitmotifs (one per villager), all in dawn-arrangement only (single season of coverage; full 4-season variants are post-slice)
- Cliffhanger overlay: "thanks for playing — please wishlist" message at Ari × Zhan first-kiss approach
- Cast representation: Ari (non-binary), Zhan (trans-male), Cyrus (blind seer) — all present without ceremony per LGBTQ+ + disability representation principle (SCOPE.md row 2026-05-10)
- Covert-guidance Sprinkles fruit-ripening chain example as Phase 4/6 template for "wisp covertly guides villager to Cyrus" mechanic
