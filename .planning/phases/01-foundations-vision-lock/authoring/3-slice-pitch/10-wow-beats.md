# Wow Beats — Colisi Slice

> 10 wow beats extracted from the slice scenario (`09-slice-walkthrough.md`). Each anchored on a Daniel-authored slice moment; no marketing-only invented beats (per `01-RESEARCH.md` Pitfall 6 + HEALTH-01 scope discipline).

### Wow beat 1 — Sterling revealed at dawn

**What happens:** The wisp drifts above a cloud barrier, learning controls through self-generated cloud-ring play. After three cycles, it notices a faint glow piercing the clouds below. The wisp dips through the cloud cover and Sterling rises into view — a small village nestled between the ocean and the mountains, bathed in dawn light. The village glow expands as the wisp descends, revealing rooftops, smoke from morning hearths, and the silhouettes of villagers beginning their day.

**Why it's trailer-screenshot-worthy:** Establishes the entire emotional register of the game in a single image — magical wonder + cozy village + golden hour aesthetic. A wishlist visitor sees this beat and knows immediately what kind of game this is. Strong "I want to be there" pull. The descent moment specifically (clouds parting, village rising) is cinematic and screenshot-perfect.

**System support (REQ refs):** ONB-01..04 (tutorial), CTRL-01..02 (controls + camera), WORLD-01..03 (zones + atmospheric world + cosmetic day/night), AUDIO-01 (dawn-arrangement leitmotif).

### Wow beat 2 — First contact with Cyrus (the only one who sees you)

**What happens:** The wisp drifts toward the Elder's Abode in Sterling. Inside, Cyrus — ancient, blind — turns his head as the wisp enters. He cannot see, but he *feels* the wisp's presence via psychic knowledge. He speaks the first words of the game: a greeting addressed to the wisp specifically, acknowledging its existence in a world that hasn't been able to. The first branching dialog opens.

**Why it's trailer-screenshot-worthy:** Cyrus's blindness + psychic knowledge is a strong cast hook ("the blind seer who knows what no one else does"). The "only one who can see you" mechanic is distinctive — every cozy-game player has wondered "why does this NPC see me?" — and Colisi's answer is diegetically rich. Screenshot: Cyrus turning his head toward the wisp, his unseeing eyes lifted, his weathered face softened with recognition.

**System support (REQ refs):** NPC-01..05, NARR-01..02, COMP-02, D-VISION-12 (spritesight mechanic introduced).

---

### Wow beat 3 — The spritesight gift ceremony

**What happens:** A villager — Sprinkles, Ari, or Zhan, in whatever order the player chooses — arrives at the Elder's Abode. Cyrus places his hand near the villager's face. A gentle pulse of psychic light passes from Cyrus to the villager. The villager blinks, looks around, and — for the first time — sees the wisp drifting in the room. Their face changes: recognition, wonder, sometimes laughter.

**Why it's trailer-screenshot-worthy:** Ritual moment with visible magical energy + a villager's reaction shot the first time they perceive the wisp. The "moment of being seen" is universally resonant. Player-driven order across playthroughs means three different first-sighting reactions are possible (Sprinkles delighted, Ari surprised, Zhan quietly moved) — replayability + emotional variety. Each first-sighting reaction can carry its own leitmotif fragment.

**System support (REQ refs):** NPC-01..05, NARR-01, COMP-01..02, AUDIO-01 (character-coded leitmotif fires here), D-VISION-12 (spritesight bestowal mechanic).

---

### Wow beat 4 — Covert-guidance fruit-ripening chain

**What happens:** The wisp catalyzes a chain of micro-actions to bring a villager to Cyrus. Sprinkles and Ari are friends; Ari occasionally wanders near the Elder's Abode. The wisp ripens a fruit Sprinkles previously mentioned — extra plump, extra appealing. Ari notices the fruit and mentions it to Sprinkles. Sprinkles walks toward the Elder's Abode to retrieve it. As Sprinkles approaches, the wisp drifts to Cyrus and signals him: someone is coming.

**Why it's trailer-screenshot-worthy:** Demonstrates the wisp's catalytic role visually through chained micro-actions rather than direct manipulation. The fruit-ripening shot (close-up: wisp drifting over the fruit; fruit visibly ripens) is a clean, magical, peaceful screenshot. The chain itself is unusually patient and gentle for a "magic helper" mechanic — wishlist visitors who've played other cozy games haven't seen this pattern before. Strong differentiation moment.

**System support (REQ refs):** NPC-01..02 (NPC schedules), NPC-09 (NPC-to-NPC gossip — Ari mentioning the fruit to Sprinkles uses the gossip layer), CRAFT-01 (gather resources — wisp catalyzes the fruit's ripeness), WORLD-02 (zones persist player changes — the fruit's altered state).

### Wow beat 5 — Magic-tutoring minigame in progress

**What happens:** The wisp catalyzes a villager mid-craft. Multiple variants possible (player choice of which to feature first depends on spritesight gift order): Sprinkles baking with magical timing-cues (cooking); Ari brewing a potion with magical ingredient-mixing (alchemy); Zhan joining wood with magical rhythm-strikes (construction); Cyrus reading a pattern with psychic-knowledge cues (psychic minigame). The wisp pulses gently with each correct beat. The villager's craft visibly transforms — the bread rises perfectly, the potion glows the right color, the joint holds without nails, the pattern resolves into meaning.

**Why it's trailer-screenshot-worthy:** Shows the player's primary moment-to-moment interaction loop in action — the player IS the magic catalyzing the villager's work. Strong "this is what you'll actually be doing" beat. Visually, the wisp + villager + glowing craft is a clean composition. Four flavor variants (cooking / alchemy / construction / psychic) means the trailer can cycle through different minigames showing the variety.

**System support (REQ refs):** NPC-01..05, NARR-01, CRAFT-01..05 (gather + craft + recipes via narrative + NarrativeMeta + gift), COMP-02 (companion contextual dialogue), D-VISION-04 (matchmaking puzzle layer — magic-tutoring is the moment-to-moment surface).

### Wow beat 6 — Villagers offer the wisp a transformation choice

**What happens:** A villager's arc reaches a milestone — perhaps Sprinkles has helped Ari open up, or Zhan has finally finished a piece he's been struggling with. The villagers gather in the Town Center. They present the wisp with a choice: in honor of what just happened, they want to transform a shared space. Options appear visually — "should we build a fountain, or set a bench grove?" The wisp's choice is committed; villagers begin the work; Sterling's Town Center will be permanently changed.

**Why it's trailer-screenshot-worthy:** Shows player agency at the meta-mechanic level (village-as-canvas per D-VISION-10). The visible choice screen + village reaction is a strong "you shape the world" beat. Differentiates from cozy games where the player owns a private space — here, the player shapes communal space, *earned* by character work.

**System support (REQ refs):** WORLD-01..02 (zones + persistent changes), HOME-01..05 (under D-VISION-10 village-as-canvas reinterpretation — 50+ shared-space upgrade slots full game), NPC-04 (arc state machine — milestone triggers the choice), D-VISION-10 Layer 2 (village-as-canvas).

### Wow beat 7 — A faerie house gifted to the wisp

**What happens:** A villager whose arc has fully resolved (one of Sprinkles / Ari / Zhan, depending on player choice) places a small, themed faerie house in their workspace or near their home. The faerie house is unique to that villager — Sprinkles's might be candy-cottage shaped; Ari's might be a hollowed gourd with potion-bottle motifs; Zhan's might be a tiny wooden cabin perfectly joined. The wisp can enter; the faerie house becomes a new rest location.

**Why it's trailer-screenshot-worthy:** Persona-zero (Dawn) contributed this mechanic — strong design pedigree. Visually distinctive (12-15 unique themed designs full game). Emotional payoff for character development work — every faerie house is a relationship record. Shows the wisp's evolving home over the course of play. Cozy-aesthetic perfect.

**System support (REQ refs):** HOME-01..05 (under D-VISION-10 Layer 3 reinterpretation — faerie houses as per-villager arc-completion themed instances), NPC-04 (arc completion triggers faerie house unlock), D-VISION-10 Layer 3.

### Wow beat 8 — Stray black cat discovered

**What happens:** The wisp drifts past a corner of Sterling — under a bench, behind the Apothecary, in an alleyway — and notices a small black cat. The cat looks up. It doesn't run. The wisp drifts closer; the cat watches. A naming prompt appears for the player. The cat now belongs to the player's playthrough — but the cat's full arc (finding it an owner; imbuing it with magic) doesn't complete in slice.

**Why it's trailer-screenshot-worthy:** Cozy-archetype perfect (the mysterious black cat). Player-named = player ownership (high-engagement design move). Discovery moment = wonder. Dawn's pet-adoption emergent-feedback origin = strong persona-zero alignment. The cat's appearance is also slice replayability — players can miss it or find it depending on their exploration path.

**System support (REQ refs):** NPC-06 (under D-VISION-11 reinterpretation — discoverable optional companion), SAVE-04 (player-named cat persists per playthrough), D-VISION-11 (stray black cat + animal rescue meta-loop anchor type).

### Wow beat 9 — Ari × Zhan compatibility surfacing

**What happens:** Ari and Zhan, both newly sighted, notice each other across Sterling. Their crafts hint at collaborative possibility (Ari brewing a remedy for nervous hands; Zhan crafting a small wooden hedge-frame). The wisp observes them seeing each other — there's a moment of recognition. Cyrus or the wisp's internal narration acknowledges what just happened: "Ah. That could be something." The matchmaking attempt becomes available.

**Why it's trailer-screenshot-worthy:** The chemistry-noticing beat is universally recognized — every romantic narrative has this moment. Visually: Ari at the Apothecary doorway, Zhan working at Carv, eyes meeting across the Town Center. Quiet, contemplative, emotionally legible. Cozy aesthetic perfect. Persona-zero-resonant (BG3 × Sims companion arcs).

**System support (REQ refs):** NPC-03 (multi-axis relationships), NPC-07 (NPCs reference past player choices), NPC-09 (NPC-to-NPC gossip), NARR-01..03 (branching dialog + persistence + multi-thread), COMP-01..04 (companion mechanics), D-VISION-04 (matchmaking puzzle), D-VISION-13 (any-to-any matchmaking with quality-based outcomes).

### Wow beat 10 — The cliffhanger first kiss

**What happens:** The slice's climax. Ari and Zhan, both growing into themselves under the wisp's catalyzing presence across ~3 days, move to share their first kiss. The camera frames them; the wisp drifts in the background, gentle witness. As their lips approach, the screen text appears: **"Thanks for playing — please wishlist."** The slice ends mid-kiss-approach. The kiss itself is unseen.

**Why it's trailer-screenshot-worthy:** The slice's wishlist-conversion mechanism is *built into* the climax beat itself. A wishlist visitor watching the trailer who sees this moment will immediately feel the pull — "I want to see the kiss complete; I want to know what happens to these two." The unfinished kiss is a structural cliffhanger. Combined with the explicit wishlist text overlay = direct conversion pressure at the moment of emotional peak. This is the slice's most important wow beat.

**System support (REQ refs):** NARR-04 (multiple satisfying endings — this kiss is the high-compat outcome), COMP-01..04, NPC-03..05 + NPC-07..09, D-VISION-04 + D-VISION-13 (matchmaking puzzle + any-to-any).
